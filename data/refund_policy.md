# ShopWave E-Commerce — Official Refund & Returns Policy
**Version 3.2 | Effective Date: January 1, 2025**

---

## 1. STANDARD RETURN WINDOW

- **Domestic Orders**: Items may be returned within **30 calendar days** of the confirmed delivery date.
- **International Orders**: Items may be returned within **14 calendar days** of the confirmed delivery date.
- The return window begins on the delivery date shown in the tracking system, not the order date.
- Requests submitted after these windows are **automatically ineligible** unless a specific exception in Section 6 applies.

---

## 2. ELIGIBLE CONDITIONS FOR RETURN

To qualify for a refund, the item must meet ONE of the following conditions:

| Condition Code | Description | Refund Type |
|---|---|---|
| `WRONG_ITEM` | Customer received an item different from what was ordered | Full refund + free return label |
| `DAMAGED_ARRIVAL` | Item arrived visibly damaged or broken (must report within **72 hours** of delivery) | Full refund OR replacement |
| `DEFECTIVE` | Item is defective/non-functional within the **90-day warranty window** | Full refund OR replacement |
| `CHANGE_OF_MIND` | Customer no longer wants the item (within 30-day window only) | Refund minus **15% restocking fee** |
| `NOT_AS_DESCRIBED` | Item significantly differs from product description/photos | Full refund + free return label |
| `MISSING_PARTS` | Item arrived with components missing | Full refund OR send missing parts |

---

## 3. NON-REFUNDABLE ITEMS (ABSOLUTE — NO EXCEPTIONS)

The following categories are **strictly non-refundable** regardless of circumstance:

1. **Digital Products** — Software licenses, e-books, downloadable content, gift cards, activation codes
2. **Final Sale / Clearance Items** — Items explicitly marked "Final Sale", "As-Is", or "Clearance" at time of purchase
3. **Perishable Goods** — Food, flowers, plants, or any item with an expiration date
4. **Personal Hygiene Products** — Once opened: underwear, swimwear, earrings, cosmetics
5. **Customized / Personalized Items** — Engraved, monogrammed, or made-to-order products
6. **Hazardous Materials** — Batteries (standalone), chemicals, flammable items

---

## 4. PARTIAL REFUNDS & BUNDLE RETURNS

- **Bundle Returns**: If a bundled product is partially returned, the refund is calculated as the **proportional value** of the returned item(s) based on the original bundle price, minus any applicable restocking fee.
- **Damaged but Functional**: If an item has cosmetic damage but is fully functional, a partial refund of **up to 30% of item value** may be issued at agent discretion.
- **Missing Accessories**: Refunds for items returned without original accessories are reduced by the accessory's retail value.

---

## 5. SUBSCRIPTION ORDERS

- **Cancellation within 24 hours** of initial charge: Full refund, no questions asked.
- **Cancellation after 24 hours**: No refund for the current billing period. Subscription will not renew.
- **Duplicate charges** due to system error: Full refund of duplicate amount.

---

## 6. SPECIAL EXCEPTION CATEGORIES

### 6a. VIP Customers (Loyalty Tier: Gold or Platinum)
- VIP customers who exceed the 30-day window by **up to 15 days** may be eligible for **store credit** (not cash refund).
- VIP exceptions MUST be **escalated to a human agent** — the AI agent cannot auto-approve VIP exceptions.

### 6b. Delivery Exceptions
- If the carrier's tracking shows "Delivered" but the customer claims non-receipt, the case must be **escalated to a human agent** for carrier investigation. Do not approve or deny.

### 6c. Medical / Accessibility Returns
- Items returned due to documented medical necessity (e.g., allergic reaction, accessibility mismatch) are eligible for full refund regardless of category (except digital). Must be **escalated for verification**.

---

## 7. FRAUD PREVENTION & ABUSE POLICY

The following triggers an **automatic denial + escalation**:

- Customer has submitted **3 or more refund requests in any rolling 90-day period**
- Customer has a **previous fraud flag** on their account (`fraud_flag: true` in CRM)
- Order shows signs of **wardrobing** (purchase near a major event, return after use)
- Refund requested for an item that was already **previously refunded**

---

## 8. HIGH-VALUE ORDER ESCALATION

- Any refund request for an order with a **total value exceeding $500** must be **escalated to a human agent**.
- This applies even if all other policy criteria are met.

---

## 9. REFUND PROCESSING TIMELINES

| Method | Processing Time |
|---|---|
| Original payment method | 5–7 business days after approval |
| Store credit | Instant upon approval |
| Gift card replacement | 1–2 business days |

---

## 10. ITEMS REQUIRING PHYSICAL RETURN

- Most items require the customer to ship the item back before a refund is processed.
- **Exceptions** (no return required, refund issued immediately):
  - `WRONG_ITEM` orders under $50
  - `DAMAGED_ARRIVAL` with photo evidence submitted
  - Items where return shipping cost exceeds item value

---

## 11. AGENT DECISION AUTHORITY

| Decision | Authority |
|---|---|
| Approve refund ≤ $500, within policy | AI Agent (autonomous) |
| Deny refund (policy violation) | AI Agent (autonomous) |
| Approve VIP exception | Human Agent required |
| High-value order (> $500) | Human Agent required |
| Fraud suspicion | Human Agent required |
| Non-delivery dispute | Human Agent required |
| Medical/accessibility exception | Human Agent required |
